### Reflection Questions and Answers

**1. Did your estimations reflect the actual effort?**  
Our estimates were fairly close, but some tasks took longer than expected because of testing and revisions.  
We learned that unfamiliar or complex tasks need more time and should include a buffer.  
Future estimates can improve by breaking large tasks into smaller, clearer steps.

**2. Was your backlog well-prioritized?**  
The backlog was mostly prioritized by importance and urgency.  
This helped us focus on the essential tasks first and keep the sprint goal in view.  
We could improve by reviewing priorities more often as requirements or progress change.

**3. How did your simulated sprint align with your plan?**  
The sprint generally followed the plan, and we completed most of the work we had selected.  
A few tasks needed adjustments when they took longer than expected.  
Regular progress checks helped us stay focused and adapt where necessary.

**4. What insights did the burndown chart give about your team's capacity?**  
The chart showed how quickly the team completed work compared with the remaining workload.  
It helped us see whether our pace was steady and whether the sprint goal was achievable.  
We can use this information to choose a more realistic amount of work in future sprints.
